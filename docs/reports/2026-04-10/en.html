Source: [Cointelegraph] | Date: 2026-04-08

Core Content: The FDIC's proposed rules will provide insurance for corporate deposits held by stablecoin issuers. Industry Impact: This regulatory framework could lead to increased scrutiny of stablecoin issuers, compelling them to adopt more rigorous financial practices.
