2. [US Proposes First Stablecoin Licensing Rules Under GENIUS Act]

Source: FinanceFeeds | Date: February 12, 2026

Core Content: The proposed rules under the GENIUS Act establish a licensing regime for stablecoin issuers, requiring them to meet specific capital and reserve requirements. This move is designed to ensure that stablecoins are backed by sufficient assets, thereby enhancing their credibility and stability in the market.

Industry Impact: The introduction of these licensing rules is a critical step towards formalizing the stablecoin market in the U.S. It aims to provide clarity for issuers and users alike, potentially fostering greater trust and encouraging more participants to enter the market. This regulatory clarity could also lead to increased institutional investment in stablecoins.
