**Source**: [Cointelegraph] | **Date**: 2026-04-08

**Background**: The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) has proposed new regulations under the GENIUS Act that will impact how stablecoin issuers manage corporate deposits.

**Core Content**: The FDIC's proposed rules will provide insurance for corporate deposits held by stablecoin issuers. However, this insurance will not extend to individual stablecoin holders, as it could create conflicts with existing banking regulations. The aim is to ensure that stablecoin issuers maintain sufficient liquidity and risk management practices.

**Industry Impact**: This regulatory framework could lead to increased scrutiny of stablecoin issuers, compelling them to adopt more rigorous financial practices.
